Leadership and Communication Wing

Building confident speakers, thoughtful leaders, and a clear public voice for the Society.

Build public speaking, debate, leadership, and interpersonal skills through structured practice while managing the Society's brand, content, and outreach.

Political Wing

Making civic systems understandable, discussable, and open to responsible participation.

Educate members on governance, law, taxation, constitutional rights, and political ideology through mock parliament, MUN, and Youth Parliament.

Technical Wing

Turning the Society's ideas and events into reliable digital experiences.

Design, build, and maintain the website, dashboards, voting systems, registration systems, and other digital tools that the Society's events run on.

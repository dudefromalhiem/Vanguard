Our Story

  • Founded at NIE Mysuru by Pranay Dinakar as a multi-wing student development society
  • Built on the idea that engineers who understand the world around them are the ones who can change it
  • Started from a gap: engineering trains students to solve technical problems but rarely to speak with confidence, understand the systems they operate in, or represent themselves outside a technical setting
  • The Society exists to close that gap, training civic literacy, communication, and technical capability side by side, under one leadership

Vision

A student community at NIE Mysuru that is civically aware, confidently vocal, and technically capable, producing graduates who are both technically excellent and democratically responsible.

Mission

  • Political Wing: Educate members on governance, law, taxation, constitutional rights, and political ideology; run mock parliament, MUN, and Youth Parliament.
  • Leadership and Communication Wing: Build public speaking, debate, leadership, and interpersonal skills through structured practice; manage the Society's brand, content, and outreach.
  • Technical Wing: Design, build, and maintain the digital tools the Society's events run on, including the website, dashboards, and voting and registration systems.
  • Run cross-wing flagship events that put every skill the Society teaches on public display.

Objectives

  • Close the gap between technical training and civic literacy, communication confidence, and leadership presence
  • Give members a standing platform for governance and policy awareness, public speaking, and technical skill-building, not just one in isolation
  • Run signature events such as Parliament Week, Constitution Day Special, Election Watch, MUNs, and inter-college competitions
  • Stay minimal on resource dependency while compounding value across all wings

Code of Conduct

  • The Society is non-partisan: no campaigning or promotion of any political party under its banner
  • All discussions are conducted respectfully; disagreements are settled through evidence, reasoning, and structured debate, not personal attacks
  • Members are encouraged to present all sides of an argument before sharing personal opinions
  • Discussions of sensitive historical events must be handled with academic rigour and appropriate sensitivity
  • The Society strictly adheres to NIE's Student Code of Conduct at all times
  • Attendance and participation are encouraged; consistent non-participation may result in membership review

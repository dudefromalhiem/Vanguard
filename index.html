The Vanguard Society

A prestigious student-led forum for debate, leadership, research, and public speaking at NIE Mysuru, shaping the next generation of innovators.

Founded in 2026, The Vanguard Society serves as the premier platform for intellectual discourse and technical innovation at our institution. We bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world leadership through our three wings: Leadership and Communication, Political, and Technical.

Our members engage in weekly parliamentary debates, publish technical research papers, and organize campus-wide hackathons. Through rigorous peer review and public speaking forums, we empower students to become thought leaders in their respective fields.

Three Wings, One Society

Every wing develops a different capability. Together, they prepare members to understand systems, communicate clearly, and build useful solutions.

Leadership and Communication

Practice public speaking, debate, leadership, interpersonal skills, content, and outreach.

Political

Study governance, law, taxation, constitutional rights, and political ideology through structured discussion.

Technical

Design and maintain the websites, dashboards, voting systems, and registration tools behind Society activities.

Learn by Taking Part

The Vanguard Society

A prestigious student-led forum for debate, leadership, research, and public speaking at NIE Mysuru, shaping the next generation of innovators.

Founded in 2026, The Vanguard Society serves as the premier platform for intellectual discourse and technical innovation at our institution. We bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world leadership through our two core divisions: The Leadership Wing and The Technical Wing.

Our members engage in weekly parliamentary debates, publish technical research papers, and organize campus-wide hackathons. Through rigorous peer review and public speaking forums, we empower students to become thought leaders in their respective fields.
